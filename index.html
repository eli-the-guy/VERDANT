Simulation controls

At 1×, one real second equals one in-game hour. Higher speeds scale proportionally. At 0× the world is paused. Arrow keys / WASD pan, + / − zoom.

Plant something

Choose something, then click the ground in the scene. Plants near ponds get extra water; fungi need damp soil and something to decompose.

Challenge mode

Resets the world, locks time at 0.1× and gives you $400. Pay to skip time (1 h $20, 5 h $30, 1 day $100) or to unlock faster speeds ($50 per +0.5×). Seeds and tools cost money — pick fruit, sell your harvest, dig up plants with the shovel to sell them. A chimera seed costs $10,000. Goal: $100,000.
